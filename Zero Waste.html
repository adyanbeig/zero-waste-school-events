Advocate Zero Waste at School Events

PTA meetings • School discos • Football matches • Community days

Why it matters

Events = Bigger Footprint

School events bring families and the whole community together—so they can also create more food waste, plastic, and trash. We can make events fun and kind to the planet.

Goal: “Leave memories, not mess.”
Step 1

Plan Food to Stop Waste

  • Count guests (students + families).
  • Serve the right amount (not too much).
  • Offer smaller portions first, then refill if needed.
  • Keep a “leftover table” for unopened items.

Tip: Use RSVP forms to estimate food numbers.

Step 2

Choose Better Packaging

  • Pick recyclable packaging made from recycled content.
  • Choose items with least packaging.
  • Use condiment dispensers instead of tiny packets.
  • Buy in bulk with compostable or recyclable wrappers.

Bonus: Clean wrappers can be reused for crafting activities!

Student Power

What Students Can Do

  • Bring a reusable water bottle.
  • Use a reusable lunch box or cutlery.
  • Volunteer as a “Zero-Waste Helper” during events.
  • Remind others politely: “Recycle here, compost here.”
